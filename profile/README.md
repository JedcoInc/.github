## Precision manufacturing for aerospace and defense

Based in **Grand Rapids, Michigan**, JEDCO manufactures engineered sheet metal components and assemblies for aircraft, gas turbine engines, commercial aerospace, and defense applications. Incorporated in **1972**, our company brings together engineering experience, specialized manufacturing processes, and a commitment to customer service.

### Our capabilities

| Metal forming | Fabrication & machining | Inspection & testing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hot forming and superplastic forming | Sheet metal fabricated assemblies | Fluorescent penetrant inspection |
| Draw, deep-draw, hydro, and spin forming | TIG, resistance, laser, and plasma welding | X-ray inspection |
| Press-brake and ring-expand forming | Machining and titanium fabrications | Pressure testing |
